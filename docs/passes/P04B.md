## Bounded change

Only `src/game/LabCamera.js` changes runtime behavior. An oblique portal transfer enables composition recovery until the already existing transported up-axis settles within0.01rad of upright. A flat transfer, reset or transform without an exit normal clears the mode. Its lifetime does not depend on room index, stage flags or an elapsed-time trigger. It intentionally survives discard-plane retirement; it never ignores a wall because this mode is active.

While this mode is active, avoidance candidates are scored at three vertical subject anchors, with modest horizontal/vertical margins. This is a cheap scoring envelope, not a claim to bound every animation exactly; acceptance measures actual deformed vertices. The search adds short horizontal-forward offsets0.6/1.2m and one intermediate upper height3m, plus elevated lateral candidates when needed. Previous-eye distance gets more weight to discourage unnecessary shoulder exchange. Every candidate and final eye retains the same collision/near-plane sweeps. Outside this geometric recovery the old candidate sets, weights and pivot score remain.

No change to mouse yaw/pitch mapping, look target construction, FOV goals, portal rigid transform, world clipping, actor visibility/materials, meshes, physics, velocities, collider geometry, brake/load rules, saves or level catalog. P07V01, P02, P03A/P03B and P04A remain. P01/PR36 is separate.
