# P04A — continuous inclined exit lens

Base: published `909882910e4d2fcab82c9af8407344dba653f98c`, tree `63345612d7970d8bef41f687f336669c281178b1`. This is the first bounded camera increment under master specification §5.2/§9.1/§11 and issue42, not closure of the entire P04 or level21 acceptance.

## Reproduced defect and scope

The rigidly transformed lens starts behind an inclined exit while the actor is emerging. The main renderer already discards the back half-space. Camera boom rays still collided with portions of the enclosing geometry in that discarded half-space; the gravity-floor safety clamp simultaneously pushed the eye up toward the upright actor. This prematurely retired the transition plane and folded the boom into/through the visible skin. Actual deformed-mesh measurements, not just head anchors, confirmed the loss.

The bounded fix makes an **inclined trailing lens** ignore only intersections behind its already active world-space discard plane. It defers the ordinary floor-height clamp only during that same state. At plane retirement it re-sweeps the final eye in the new render state, in the same update. This last check prevents a one-frame collision gap (0.124 m in the rejected intermediate trace).

Scope is geometric (an active exit with an oblique normal), never a room number, stage flag, actor ID or time-based teleport. Flat floor/wall passages keep the prior camera behavior. An unscoped prototype failed the retained room14 visibility regression; it was rejected rather than changing that test. Two earlier framing/pivot experiments also failed the actual skin measure and are not in the patch.

## Protected behavior

Only `LabCamera.js` changes runtime behavior. No geometry, collider, physical actor, portal transform, speed, force, mouse yaw/pitch mapping, FOV target, animation, material, opacity, source GLB, save or level catalog changes. All foreground surfaces still participate in the camera sweep. No wall is removed from the scene. Normal clipping of the part of the character that is actually crossing the portal remains intentional and is measured separately.

## Evidence contract

The whole route reaches joint victory without any test position assignment. Synthetic wall fixtures are labelled separately.
- The emerging-lens acceptance window is the first **0.4 s** after each inclined transfer. Both the solo and held-companion transfer must retain more than500 uncut source vertices and lose none of those vertices outside the viewport. Every final lens pose during the recorded2 s must pass its near-plane sweep in the current clipping state.
- Existing camera regressions include rooms12/13/14, real aiming geometry, no shot kick, rigid transform continuity and update-rate stability. Full campaign CI remains required, not inferred from those targeted results.
- `scripts/p04-exit-camera-browser.mjs` executes a complete ordinary route in actual Chromium, records short consecutive native windows around both inclined exits, and preserves per-frame lens/actor state, current clipping planes and deformed skin bounds. All scene geometry and the ordinary camera remain present. Each clip is1280×720,30encoded frames/s,60Hz display/120Hz physics, **without audio**. This is not a full-level video, hardware FPS or human solution time.
- Baseline and candidate are built separately from immutable revisions with the same recording script and dependency lock. Reports retain source commit, input digest, frame sequence and video SHA-256. A workflow definition or queued run is not evidence of success; concrete results belong in the PR and delivery report.

## Remaining known limitation

There is a second, later framing defect near the upper aperture: in the present diagnostic route, five display frames about0.55–0.63 s after the joint transfer lose part of the upper skin. It occurs after the discard plane is gone, at an ordinary collision-avoidance change. P04A does not hide, omit from recording, or claim to fix it. Address that separately as P04B with the same complete route and foreground-wall safety checks. The console-side obstruction is also separate. Issue42 and W03 must remain open.

Local browser navigation is denied by the environment's administrator policy. That policy is not modified; native rendering runs in the existing GitHub Actions environment. Publication must retain every normal check and confirm the actual public build before being reported as live.
