# P09B — campaign-wide portal pipeline, not one room

User request: optimize the whole game and portals/traversal, inspect previously unprofiled paths and inconsistencies. Base `c47364e8ec30872ed26dd64cbe2c852fcf2e7456`, exact tree `8acf42e0b53910795c8ffb9b2158ac0787793504`. Master specification sections 2, 9, 11 and 14 apply. Desktop/Android frame budgets remain targets, not verified guarantees on an unnamed device.

## Four shared changes

1. **Portal rendering:** the existing scissor limited pixels but not scene submissions. `LabPortalRenderCulling` conservatively excludes rigid leaf meshes wholly outside the same aperture rectangle, with a two-pixel guard. Its cropped matrix is used ONLY to cull: the actual optical projection, full viewport, shader UVs, HDR, MSAA, recursion, resolution and every-frame refresh are unchanged. It uses the same aggregate renderer bounds for instancing. Skinning, morph/displacement/custom shaders, render callbacks, never-culled objects and meshes with descendants keep the full path. Visibility and scene matrix-update policy are restored in `finally`. A forced shadow refresh bypasses culling so casters are not removed. This is not guessed occlusion or lower quality.
2. **Backing-wall queries during movement/carry/portal contacts:** execute the exact existing world-axis rejection before creating a portal-local eight-corner box. Acceptance predicate and tolerances remain the same. No nearby wall or capsule check is skipped by an arbitrary distance threshold.
3. **Repeated portal synchronization:** reuse the last computed frame only when the complete anchor transform, local anchor values, resulting frame and group pose are unchanged. Moving/rotating supports, render interpolation and external frame/anchor edits invalidate it. Physics snapshots still commit independently each fixed step; no interpolation sample becomes physical history.
4. **Repeated shots / first-use resources:** keep two bounded portal visual slots. Replacing/clearing a portal no longer disposes and recreates its three geometries/materials. `prepare()` attaches the two hidden visuals during the existing loading/compile phase, without making portals logically present. Compile-only nodes are detached in `finally` afterwards (including a compilation failure); the bounded resources, not extra scene traversal, are retained. A new placement still creates a fresh logical frame and anchor/history. Clearing detaches both visuals; final disposal releases all six geometries/materials and three render targets exactly once. This does not claim that every possible driver shader variant or upload stall is eliminated.

Runtime files: `LabPortals.js`, new `LabPortalRenderCulling.js`, and two `LabGame.js` calls to prepare during loading. No original model, material appearance, light, quality default, room geometry, collider shape, portal size/direction, velocity/impulse, controller, save or puzzle rule change. P09A camera broad phase and the readable receiver remain. The separate camera-continuity defect is not fixed by this optimization.

## Audit and remaining scope

The initial complete CPU audit also highlights room10 and contact-heavy mechanisms: their cost is not identical to portal rendering. Repeated static-mask setters waking cargo and the remaining interpolation/first-use variants warrant separate investigation rather than silently changing sleep/physics semantics in an optimization-only patch. No claim that every bug is found, every memory counter plateaus, or every device has zero dropped frames. Device model/GPU/browser and real frame traces are still required by the specification. Do not turn fixed-simulation movie fps or fewer triangles into that guarantee.

The first full-suite candidate failed nine existing scene-workload contracts: hidden compile-only portal nodes remained attached after loading. The retained fix detaches them in `finally` after compilation, rather than weakening those existing tests or excluding them from counts. It adds a dedicated lifecycle regression. Full-suite status is rechecked on this corrected tree.

The extended replace-during-transit regression exposed a candidate-only lifetime hazard: pooling the scene root could resurrect an old camera exit-plane reference when that slot was reused. The retained implementation pools GPU meshes/materials but creates a fresh lightweight logical root per placement; the old frame root stays detached. The regression fails on the first pooled version and passes after the fix. This is not presented as a pre-existing released-game bug.

A secondary-render-pass audit added a conservative bypass for transmissive materials and scene override materials. Refraction may sample full-view background outside the aperture, so those scenes must not use visibility-based cropping. All21 current campaign scenes and imported assets were scanned: no active transmissive mesh was found (an unused material factory is not a rendered mesh). The fallback protects future/material-review configurations without lowering glass quality. A regression verifies off-aperture background remains submitted.
