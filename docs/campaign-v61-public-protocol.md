## Scope

These are finite automated ordinary-input observations on software WebGL.
Complete route recordings and finite attacks do not establish human discovery,
absence of every possible bypass, hardware FPS, blind playtesting, all-frame
subjective camera quality, live Yandex SDK/advertising or moderation acceptance.
The castle still has 12 mechanisms; neither 500 unique stages nor a human
15-minute speedrun is claimed.
